How we protect data

Security we can show you, not just claim.

Here is exactly what protects your data today — and what is still on our roadmap. We would rather show you the current state than advertise a certification we have not been audited for.

In active development

These controls are built and shipping in the current release.

  • TLS 1.3 on every request
  • Encrypted at rest on all volumes
  • Per-file access control
  • Session revocation and device management
  • NDPR-aligned data residency (NG)

Known gaps

Published deliberately. The vault currently stores credentials without client-side envelope encryption, and ISO 27001 certification is not held. Both are on the 2026 roadmap.

Last reviewed: October 2026

Data residency

Your data stays in Nigerian regions. No cross-border replication without your consent.

Audit trail

Every administrative action is recorded with actor and timestamp.
