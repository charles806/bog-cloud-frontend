About BOG Cloud

BOG Cloud is a Nigerian cloud platform for file storage, credential management and deployment, built and operated by Greenxchange Tech Lab in Lagos. We operate data residency in Lagos and Abuja, price in naira, and publish our gaps as well as our wins.

Our principles

  • Data stays in Nigeria — no cross-border replication without consent.
  • Honest status — degraded means degraded; incidents are published.
  • One bill — Drive, Vault and Deploy share one invoice and one login.
